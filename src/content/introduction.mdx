CmdRound is a platform that assists hiring managers or recruiters who are looking out for
candidates that are great at using agents to build.

## Who it's for

It's for:

- Recruiters who want to find engineers who have outsourced speed and efficiency to
  artificial intelligence, but who will always internalise the understanding and
  implementation of complex problems and solutions.

  Recruiters create an assessment on CmdRound and send it to the candidates. The
  candidate takes the assessment using a built-in agent, and all of their work with that
  agent is recorded and sent back to the recruiter.

- Candidates, who receive a unique link to accept and take an assessment offered by the
  hiring organisation. The assessment comes with a task or a set of tasks, a goal to
  reach and an agent to build or fix whatever the tasks require. After submission, they
  are assured that their score and activity have been sent over to the hiring managers,
  and they will take it from there.

## How it works

1. Create an assessment after purchasing a pack that fits your hiring need.
2. Import your repo or use any of our tailor-made tasks that fit the role you are hiring
   for.
3. The assessment is sent to the candidate's email. You are notified at every point:
   when they accept the assessment, when they begin and when they conclude.
4. On your dashboard you see their score. Our scoring guide is
   [here](https://cmdround.com/scoring). You can also watch the replay of the
   candidate's work. See what prompts they used, what permissions they granted the agent
   and other pointers that help you make a hiring decision.
