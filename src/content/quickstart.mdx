---

By the end of this page you'll be equipped to send your first assessment to a candidate
using our tailor-made tasks.

## Before you start

- A CmdRound account. Create one [here](https://cmdround.com/start), or if you already
  have one, continue [here](https://cmdround.com/dashboard).
- A seat pack that matches your hiring need. We have the Starter, Team and Scale packs.
  You buy it while making your first assessment.
- And of course, your candidates' email addresses.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up">
    Sign up at [cmdround.com/start](https://cmdround.com/start), with Google or with an
    email.

    ![The CmdRound sign-up page with the seat packs and the sign-up buttons](/images/signup.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a new assessment">
    On your dashboard, click "New assessment".

    ![The dashboard with the "New assessment" button](/images/dashboard-new-assessment.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Buy a pack">
    You'll be asked to purchase a pack at this stage. After paying, click "Build an
    assessment".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the details">
    Now enter the name of the assessment you are creating and your company name. For
    example:

    - Name of assessment: Snr Frontend Assessment
    - Company name: Andela Inc. (By default it uses the company you filled in when you
      created your account.)

    Now click "Next" at the bottom right of the modal.

    ![The details step with the assessment name and company filled in](/images/builder-details.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your tasks">
    The tasks section lists all 9 of our tailor-made tasks. Each one tests if a candidate
    can deliver a feature, hold context and push back when the ticket is wrong. You can
    select up to 3 tasks that match your need for this role.

    When you are done selecting, click "Next" to go to the invite section.

    ![The tasks step with two tasks picked and the task details open on the right](/images/builder-tasks.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite your candidates">
    The invite section lets you add a note to the candidates. This can be anything from
    what you expect from them to a simple instruction that increases their success with
    the task and the overall assessment.

    You're also asked to add a "Closes" date. When that date comes, the assessment
    becomes invalid for the candidates.

    Now add the candidates' emails. You can add as many as your pack allows. When you're
    done, click "Create the link".
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What success looks like

At this stage you should see "Assessment sent". Each candidate you added to this
assessment is listed with an "Emailed" tag, which means CmdRound has sent the candidate
an invite to take the assessment. You also get a "Copy invite" button, which copies a
unique link for that candidate that you can send to them directly.

When you are satisfied, click "Done" and you will be taken to your dashboard.
